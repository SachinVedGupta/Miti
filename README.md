# ABOUT

🏆 Winner at HackMIT! 🎶

I had the amazing opportunity to travel to Boston and compete in HackMIT, one of the world’s largest hackathons, hosted at MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology)! With over 1,000 talented competitors from around the globe, the experience was both eye-opening and exhilarating. I am very happy to have won the Best Music Hack @ HackMIT, which was a track sponsored by SunoAI, who awarded us each $500 in credits for their Text to Song LLM.

My team and I, all passionate about music, wanted to address the lack of innovation in the music production industry—a field that has seen a plateau due to monopolization by popular MIDI editors like FL Studio and Ableton. Inspired by our love for music generation and our interest in technology, we created "Miti" which isn't just a play on the words MIT and MIDI. Rather, "Miti" is a Collaborative AI Music Studio that aims to modernize music production and make it more accessible for beginners.

About "Miti"
- Streamlines the music production process with AI-generated song inspiration
- Easy track modification via simple prompts (e.g., "add a note every 5 seconds")
- Real-time collaboration to create music with friends

Big shoutout to Victor Tao and Jeff Gardner from SunoAI for the wonderful insight on SunoAI and the music production industry. It was also great meeting with Liam Esparraguera from Warp, Arunima Mittra from Capital One, Philip Kiely from Baseten, Colin Sidoti from Clerk.com, Hudson River Trading, and Fetch.ai.
